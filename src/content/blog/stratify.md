Ever found yourself overwhelmed by the sheer number of lists you need to keep track of? Whether it's a grocery list, a to-do list, or a bucket list, managing multiple lists can be a daunting task. That's where Stratify comes in.

## The Birth of Stratify

I created Stratify out of personal necessity. As a food enthusiast and avid traveler, I found myself constantly juggling various lists, from new restaurants to try to must-see destinations. I needed a tool that could not only help me organize my lists but also provide me with insights and prioritize tasks.

## Key Features of Stratify

- List Creation: Easily create and manage an unlimited number of lists.
- Ranking and Prioritization: Assign ranks or priorities to items within your lists to help you focus on the most important tasks.
- Sorting: Sort your lists by title or rank for a more organized view.
- Visual Cues: Add visual cues to your lists to quickly identify different categories or types.

## Real-World Use Cases

To give you a better idea of how Stratify can benefit you, let's explore some real-world use cases:

- Food Enthusiast: Create a list of new restaurants to try, rank them based on your dining experience, and sort by cuisine type.
- Wine Lover: Build a list of wines you've tasted, rate them, and sort by grape variety.
- Movie Buff: Organize your watchlist by genre or release date, and rank your favorite movies of all time.
- Traveler: Create a bucket list of places you want to visit, prioritize them based on your interests, and sort by location.

## Conclusion

Stratify is more than just a list-making app; it's a tool that empowers you to organize your life and achieve your goals. Whether you're a student, a professional, or simply someone who wants to stay organized, Stratify is here to help.

At this time, Stratify is iOS only, but we're working on an Android version. Stay tuned for updates and new features! Feel free to download Stratify from the App Store and start organizing your lists today. Happy list-making!
